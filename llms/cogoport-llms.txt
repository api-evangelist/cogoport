# Cogoport Overview > Cogoport is a global logistics and supply chain platform offering freight booking, customs, trade finance, and supply chain visibility for businesses across ocean, air, and inland transport.

## Key Features
-*Freight Booking**: Book ocean (FCL, LCL) and air freight with competitive rates.
-*Customs & Compliance**: Streamlined customs clearance solutions and support.
-*Trade Finance**: Financial services including invoice financing and pay-later solutions.
-*Rate Discovery**: Transparent real-time sea and air freight rate search.
-*Supply Chain Visibility**: End-to-end tracking and predictive logistics.
-*Documentation Support**: Automated document generation and validation tools.
-*Logistics Tools**: Access HS Code Finder, Container Trackers, Shipping Cost Calculator, and more.
